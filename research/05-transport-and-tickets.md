# Getting around & tickets — Almaty, Nov 2026

_Researched 30 Oct 2026._

## Arrival — ALA Terminal 2 (all international flights)
- SIM: 24/7 counters in T2 arrivals hall (ground floor, before exit): Kcell/Activ (eSIM on the spot) and Tele2. Tele2 tourist SIM 5,590 ₸ = 50 GB + 240 min, 30 days. Bring passport. (alaport.com, Oct 2026; asocialnomad)
- Money: 24/7 bank ATMs and exchange (Tabys, Birinshi, Yes Exchange) in T2 arrivals. Airport rates are poor — change a little; Halyk/Kaspi ATMs in the city.
- To the city: **Yandex Go** Economy ~2,500–4,500 ₸, Comfort ~3,500–6,000 ₸, 25–45 min — use the marked Yandex pickup zone. Official "Taxi Point" desk (fixed fare, ~9,000 ₸) at T2 exits A/B. **Ignore drivers who approach you (8,000–18,000 ₸ cash).**
- Bus 92 (06:00–23:00, 120 ₸) from terminal exit — needs an Onay card (cash-only machine in T2, 800 ₸ card fee). Not worth it with luggage.

## Paying for transport
- **Metro**: one line, 06:20–00:00, 120 ₸; contactless Visa/Mastercard accepted. Stations: **Abay** (Abay × Furmanov), **Almaly**, **Zhibek Zholy** (Green Bazaar / Panfilov / Arbat).
- **Buses**: 120 ₸, cashless; Onay card bought with cash only; Onay app needs a Kazakh number. Foreign bank cards don't work on most buses → use **Yandex Go**.

## Kok Tobe
- Lower station: Dostyk Ave by the Palace of the Republic (koktobe.com lists Dostyk 104B) — check in 2GIS.
- Cable car: 6,000 ₸ one-way / **10,000 ₸ return** (child 3,000/5,000). Tickets at the station.
- Hours: Mon, Wed, Thu, Sun 10:00–22:00 · **Tue 13:00–22:00** (maintenance) · Fri–Sat 10:00–23:00.
- Taxis can't drive up (25,000 ₸ car fee) — park shuttle 3,000 ₸ one-way / 4,000 ₸ return from Dostyk.
- Risk: closed early Nov–20 Nov 2025 for overhaul; call +7 727 272 77 77.

## Medeu & Shymbulak
- Medeu rink closed (reconstruction to ~Q4 2027); dam viewpoint/stairs open.
- Bus 12 up Dostyk Ave to Medeu (stop "Канатная дорога Медеу"), 120 ₸ via Onay, 30–50 min. **Taxi ~2,000–4,000 ₸** is easier for visitors.
- **Road Medeu→Shymbulak closed to all private cars incl. taxis until 30 Nov 2026** — gondola only.
- Lifts back after 1 Nov (KKD-4 chairlift to 9 Nov).
- Tickets (Sep 2026): **Medeu → Talgar Pass (3,200 m) return 12,000 ₸** · one-way 7,000 ₸ · glass "360°" cabin 14,000 ₸ · from Shymbulak base Combi-1+2 return 7,000 ₸. Buy at Medeu base ticket office or shymbulak.com.
