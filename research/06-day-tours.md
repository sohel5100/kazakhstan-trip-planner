# Day tours — Charyn, Kolsai, Kaindy & more (Nov 2026)

_Researched 30 Oct 2026 from live GetYourGuide listings (prices shown in ₹) + one blog. TripAdvisor/Viator/Reddit were blocked for the research tool._

## Why one 2-day trip, not two day trips
Kolsai & Kaindy lie beyond Charyn on the same road. A Kolsai+Kaindy-only day is still ~16 h; Charyn-only is another 8–10 h → ~25 h, mostly repeated driving. A 2-day tour uses the same two days with 2–3 h less driving per day and real time at each site — important with sunset ~16:30.

## Cash to carry on tour days (tenge)
Lunch ~5,000 ₸ · Kaindy UAZ 1,000–2,000 ₸ each way (~$8) · horses 7,000–10,000 ₸ · Charyn eco-taxi 500–1,800 ₸ · toilets 100 ₸.

## Tips from reviews
Power bank, warm layers, gloves, grippy shoes, snacks; motion-sickness tablets for the Kaindy UAZ (sit forward); walking to Kaindy safer than horses; book with free cancellation and recheck forecast the day before.
