# Where to stay — Almaty, 7–14 Nov 2026 (7 nights)

_Researched 30 Oct 2026. Prices: Trip.com dated rates for 7–14 Nov (1 adult, taxes incl.) unless noted. ~453 ₸/US$, ~5 ₸/₹._

## Areas
| Area | Good | Watch out |
|---|---|---|
| Golden Square / Dostyk–Abay | Kok Tobe station (east end of Abay), bus 12 to Medeu at Hotel Kazakhstan, Abay & Almaly metro, Opera | Pricier; uphill walks south |
| **Panfilov / Arbat (Zhibek Zholy)** | Most walkable: Arbat, Panfilov Park, Zenkov, Green Bazaar; best value | Lower = worse winter smog; street noise |
| Samal / Esentai | Upmarket, cleaner air | No metro; taxis to old centre |

Airport is ~25–30 min by car from all of these.

## Practical
- No self-registration needed for 7 nights (hotel files e-Qonaq).
- Heating is on and air is very dry — ask for a humidifier.
- Arrival ~16:30 is after check-in time everywhere.
- For the 04:30 checkout: pay the night before, ask for a breakfast box, book the taxi/hotel transfer the evening before.
