# Entry, food, money, safety & etiquette — Almaty, Nov 2026

_Researched 30 Oct 2026._

## Entry (Indian passport)
- 14 days visa-free (Govt Decree No. 464, 7 Jul 2022); max 42 days per 180; no extension. (gov.kz MFA Delhi; Indian Embassy Astana, updated 29 Oct 2026)
- No migration registration needed for stays ≤30 days; hotels notify via E-Qonaq. If using Airbnb, confirm host files it.
- Carry original passport always (police don't accept copies). Keep printouts of return ticket + hotel.
- Indian consular officer, Almaty (WhatsApp): +7 771 657 8751 · cons.almaty@mea.gov.in
- Declare cash above USD 10,000.

## Indian & vegetarian
- **Govinda's** (Abylai Khan 39): pure veg; set meal ~2,050 ₸ (2026); go mid-afternoon, sells out.
- **Magic Masala** (Abay 107a): 4.6★ (377), Jain-friendly, 11–23.
- **Tandoor** (Tole Bi 102): 4.3★ (2,474), veg thali; mixed reviews.
- Veg-friendly local: plain baursak, pumpkin samsa, pumpkin manti (ask), lepyoshka, irimshik. Ask about meat broth/fat. Beef & horse are common.

## Green Bazaar (Zhibek Zholy 53)
- ~09:00–19:00 daily (verify Monday). Dried fruit, nuts, honey, spices, kurt. Bargain on souvenirs/dried fruit; pay cash; watch for pickpockets.

## Money
- Foreigners can't use Kaspi QR (Alipay+ only); UPI not linked.
- Visa/Mastercard work widely; **assume RuPay won't**.
- ATMs: Halyk, Kaspi, Bereke (low/no fee); choose "charge in KZT".
- INR not exchangeable — bring USD $100 notes; city exchange offices beat airport by 3–5%.
- Tipping: check bill for 10–15% "обслуживание"; otherwise 5–10%. No tip for taxis.

## Safety
- Airport touts quote ~18,000 ₸ vs 3,000–5,000 ₸ in Yandex Go; never cancel app ride to pay cash.
- Fake police scams reported (Nov 2025): show passport, not wallet.
- Smog AQI 150–300+ on inversion days: N95 + IQAir.
- Emergency: 112 · police 102 · ambulance 103 · fire 101.

## Etiquette & phrases
- Kazakh: Sälemetsiz be (hello), Rakhmet (thanks). Russian: Skolko stoit? · Schyot, pozhaluysta · Ya ne yem myaso · Bez myasa.
- Zenkov Cathedral: women cover heads, no flash. Mosque: cover up, shoes off.
- No photos near military/airport/official sites. Alcohol 21+, no shop sales 23:00–08:00.

## Souvenirs
- Rakhat factory store: Zenkov 2a (cheaper than supermarkets); also Zhibek Zholy/Kaldayakov.
- Felt crafts at TSUM and Green Bazaar upper floor (slippers ~2,000–2,500 ₸, kalpak $15–30).
