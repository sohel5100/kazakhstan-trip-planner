# What travelers say — Almaty in November

_Researched 30 Oct 2026. Reddit/TripAdvisor pages were blocked for the research tool, so this draws on blogs, forums, TripAdvisor snippets and Kazakh news._

## Biggest takeaways
- **Don't do Charyn and Kolsai as two separate day trips** — Charyn is on the way to Kolsai; separate trips repeat ~6–7 h of driving. Do a **2-day Charyn → Saty (overnight) → Kolsai** trip instead (private ~320,000 ₸ for two, kaziatour.kz).
- **Never Charyn + Kolsai + Kaindy in one day** (12+ h driving).
- **Kaindy** may be unreachable: hiking trail closed Nov 2025 (Tengrinews), road being rebuilt; an Indian traveler hit heavy snow there mid-October (Goniyo forum).
- **Shymbulak sits above the smog** — do it on the clearest day; top lift last ride up ~16:00.
- **Kok Tobe is a gamble** in smog: views can vanish by afternoon (travelwithkevinandruth, Nov 2023). Go ~1 h before sunset on a clear day; maintenance on Tuesdays.

## Mistakes to avoid
- Can't buy tenge in India — bring clean USD; use Halyk/Kaspi ATMs, choose KZT.
- Kaspi needs a Kazakh bank account. Carry cash for bazaar, small cafés, park fees.
- Only Yandex Go at the airport; check the plate.
- Bus 12 is cashless (Onay card / app / QR); foreign cards often fail in Onay → taxi to Medeu is simpler. Metro accepts contactless bank cards.
- Google Maps lacks buses: use 2GIS/Yandex Maps. Download Google Translate offline Russian.
- Airport SIM ~6,000 ₸ vs ~1,500 ₸ in city — set up an eSIM before flying.
- Book tours early; many operators slow to reply in winter.

## Overrated / underrated
- Kok Tobe: mixed (price, kitsch, smog). Green Bazaar: worth it (closed Mondays per one guide). Zenkov + Panfilov: short but good, free, 8–19. Botanical Garden: underwhelming mid-Nov. Big Almaty Lake: skip in Nov.

## Suggested shape (adopted with tweaks)
Sat arrive & settle · Sun city centre + bazaar (+ Kok Tobe if clear) · Mon Shymbulak (museums closed Mon) · Tue–Wed 2-day Charyn + Kolsai · Thu buffer: Arasan + museums · Fri museum/shopping + early farewell dinner.
